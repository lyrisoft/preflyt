# Code of Conduct

Preflyt contributors and maintainers must treat people respectfully across issues, reviews, discussions and project events. Welcome different experience levels, give specific constructive feedback, respect privacy and focus disagreement on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, impersonation and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Do not use contribution discussions to attack people or pressure them to disclose personal information.

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@lyrisoft.tech** with the subject `Preflyt conduct report`. Include relevant context and links; omit unrelated sensitive information. Do not post reports publicly. The owner confirmed mailbox delivery on 16 October 2026. @iampeters currently handles reports as the sole maintainer; there is no independent backup reporting channel. If the report concerns the maintainer, identify the conflict and request independent handling. The project should arrange an independent reviewer where one is available and acceptable to the reporter; it cannot promise that reviewer in advance.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request correction, limit participation or exclude participants according to the circumstances. They should explain decisions privately where appropriate, protect reporters' privacy and consider conflicts of interest. Reports are shared only as needed to handle them; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

This policy applies to project spaces and conduct while representing Preflyt. It does not create a contractual response SLA.
